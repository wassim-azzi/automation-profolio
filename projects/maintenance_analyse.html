<h1 id="1.Data-Analyse">1.Data Analyse¶</h1><p>This section analyzes the machine operating data collected during August 2026.</p> <p>The objective is to evaluate the main machine parameters and identify abnormal operating conditions that may be associated with machine failures.</p> <p>The following parameters are analyzed:</p>
  • Temperature (°C)
  • Rotational speed (rpm)
  • Torque (Nm)
  • Machine failure status
<p>The analysis uses:</p>
  • Pandas for data loading, filtering, and manipulation.
  • NumPy for numerical calculations.
  • Matplotlib for data visualization.

<h2 id="1.4-Determination-of-Operating-Limits">1.4 Determination of Operating Limits¶</h2><p>To identify abnormal operating conditions, reference limits are determined from measurements corresponding to normal machine operation.</p> <p>Only observations where Failure = 0 are considered.</p> <p>The maximum observed value under normal operation is used as the reference limit for:</p>
  • Temperature
  • Rotational speed
  • Torque
<p>Values exceeding these limits are considered potential abnormal conditions.</p>
In [12]:
normal_data = df.loc[
    mask & (df["Failure"] == 0)
]

limit_temp = normal_data["Temperature"].max()
limit_speed = normal_data["Rotational speed"].max()
limit_torque = normal_data["Torque"].max()

<h2 id="1.5-Detection-of-Abnormal-Operating-Conditions">1.5 Detection of Abnormal Operating Conditions¶</h2><p>The previously calculated normal operating limits are used to detect measurements that exceed the expected operating range.</p> <p>An alarm is generated when:</p>
  • Temperature > normal temperature limit
  • Rotational speed > normal rotational speed limit
  • Torque > normal torque limit
<p>These alarms indicate potentially abnormal machine operating conditions.</p>
In [14]:
high_temperature = df.loc[

]

high_speed = df.loc[
    mask & (df["Rotational speed"] > limit_speed)
]

high_torque = df.loc[
    mask & (df["Torque"] > limit_torque)
]

<h3 id="Interpretation">Interpretation¶</h3><p>The comparison between rotational speed and torque shows that both parameters vary significantly during the analyzed period.</p> <p>The rotational speed generally remains around 1,400–1,600 rpm, with several peaks exceeding 2,000 rpm. These peaks indicate periods of increased rotational speed that may require further investigation.</p> <p>The torque also presents significant variations throughout the month. Since the torque values are multiplied by 30 in the graph, this scaling is used only to facilitate visual comparison and does not represent the actual torque values.</p> <p>The two parameters do not appear to have a strong direct relationship in this visualization. Several high rotational-speed values occur when the torque is relatively low, while some higher torque values occur at moderate rotational speeds.</p> <p>Therefore, rotational speed and torque should be analyzed together with other machine parameters, such as temperature and failure status, to better identify abnormal operating conditions.</p>
